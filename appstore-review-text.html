App Store Localizable Text
Read three classical instruments - barometer, thermometer, hygrometer - and predict the weather to protect your village across 50 atmospheric observation contracts.

Mercury Barometer is an offline weather forecasting simulation built with SpriteKit. You play as a village weather observer stationed at historic observation posts across the world, reading classical mercury instruments to predict the next day's weather and keep your village safe from storms.

HOW TO PLAY
- Each day the three instruments (barometer, thermometer, hygrometer) animate to new readings. Watch the needles settle.
- Read the on-screen hint text and combine it with the three readings.
- Tap one of the five weather prediction buttons: Sunny, Cloudy, Rainy, Storm, or Snowy.
- Correct prediction: village safety unchanged. Wrong prediction: village safety drops by 20%.
- Multi-day contracts (Levels 9, 20, 35, 50) require 3 or 5 consecutive daily predictions for a combined score.

50 CONTRACTS
Journey across five atmospheric observation stations, each hiding more instruments and offering more weather choices:
- Royal Society London (Levels 1-10): All three instruments visible. Learn the basics.
- Alpine Monastery (Levels 11-20): One instrument hidden. Mountain weather patterns.
- North American Plains (Levels 21-30): One instrument hidden. Severe storms and tornado risk.
- Yangtze Observatory (Levels 31-40): Two instruments hidden. Monsoon and humid conditions.
- Arctic Field Station (Levels 41-50): Two instruments hidden. Blizzard and extreme cold.

INSTRUMENT SYSTEM
- Barometer: mercury column in hPa, range 940 to 1060, silver tone.
- Thermometer: mercury column in degrees Celsius, range -25 to 48, red tone.
- Hygrometer: dial in percent relative humidity, range 0 to 100, blue tone.
- Each instrument fills with a smoothstep animation over 0.8 seconds.

WEATHER TYPES AND DIFFICULTY
- Five weather types: Sunny, Cloudy, Rainy, Storm, Snowy.
- Difficulty progression by available choices:
  - Levels 1-5: 2 weather choices.
  - Levels 6-15: 3 choices.
  - Levels 16-30: 4 choices.
  - Levels 31+: 5 choices.

SCORING
- Accuracy: (correct predictions / total predictions) x 60 = 60 max.
- Speed: max(0, 20 - total_time x 0.5) = 20 max.
- Village Safety: villageHealth x 0.2 = 20 max (starts at 100).
- Total: clamped to [0, 100].
- Stars: 3 stars (total >= 80), 2 stars (60 <= total < 80), 1 star (40 <= total < 60), 0 stars Try Again (total < 40).

FEATURES
- 50 levels across five historic weather observation stations.
- Three classical mercury instruments with realistic fill animations.
- Five weather types with progressive difficulty by available choices.
- Multi-day contracts at Levels 9, 20, 35, and 50 for combined scoring.
- Village health bar that drops on wrong predictions.
- Linear level unlock stored locally via NSUserDefaults.
- Fully offline. No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no tracking.
- Portrait orientation, iPhone only.

Mercury Barometer is a calm, atmospheric observation puzzle about reading the instruments and whispering the pressure before the storm arrives.

1. Launch the app.
2. The main menu shows "Mercury Barometer" title with "Play", "Levels", and "Settings" buttons on a deep-blue atmospheric background.
3. Tap "Play" to enter Level 1. Levels 1-5 are tutorial levels with only 2 weather choices to learn the basics.
4. The game scene shows three instruments (barometer, thermometer, hygrometer) animating to daily readings, a village with houses and trees at the bottom, a village health bar, and weather prediction buttons.
5. Read the on-screen hint text and the three instrument readings (pressure in hPa, temperature in Celsius, humidity in percent).
6. Tap one of the weather prediction buttons at the bottom: Sunny, Cloudy, Rainy, Storm, or Snowy. The number of choices depends on level (2 at L1-5, 3 at L6-15, 4 at L16-30, 5 at L31+).
7. If the prediction is correct, a green feedback text appears and village safety is unchanged. If wrong, a red feedback text appears and village safety drops by 20%.
8. Complete the level by making the required prediction(s). A result panel appears showing accuracy, speed, village safety, total score, and stars awarded (3 stars at total >= 80, 2 at >= 60, 1 at >= 40, 0 = Try Again at < 40).
9. From the result panel, tap "Next Level" to proceed.
10. Return to menu and tap "Levels" to see the level select grid. Verify locked levels cannot be opened until the previous level earns at least 1 star.
11. Progress to Level 9 or 20 to verify multi-day contracts (3 consecutive daily predictions). Levels 35 and 50 require 5 consecutive daily predictions.
12. Progress to Level 11 (Alpine Monastery) or later to verify one instrument is hidden. At Level 31 (Yangtze Observatory) or later, two instruments are hidden.
13. Tap the Settings button to verify Music and Sound Effects toggles. Note: toggles exist but no audio files are shipped in v1.0 (silent game).
14. Verify the app is fully offline: no network calls, no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases. All progress is saved locally on device via NSUserDefaults.
15. The app supports portrait orientation only and iPhone only. ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false.
