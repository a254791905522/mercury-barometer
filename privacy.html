Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Mercury Barometer ("the App") is a fully offline single-player game. This Privacy Policy describes how the App handles data.

Information Collection

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. No personal data is ever sent to any server.

Local Game Data

The App stores only game progress locally on your device using iOS standard NSUserDefaults. This includes:

  • Level stars and high scores (keys: mb_level_N_stars, mb_level_N_highscore)
  • Unlocked level count (key: mb_unlockedLevels)
  • Music and sound effect preferences (keys: mb_musicEnabled, mb_soundEnabled)

This data never leaves your device and is not backed up to any cloud service by the App.

Children's Privacy

The App is rated 4+ and contains no content unsuitable for children. The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or any other user.

Internet, Ads, and Purchases

The App does not access the internet, does not display ads, does not offer in-app purchases, and does not include any analytics or tracking SDKs.

Changes to This Policy

If the privacy practices of the App change, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
